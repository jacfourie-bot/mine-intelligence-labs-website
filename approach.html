Our Approach

Complex problems deserve better methods.

We are developing an integrated engineering methodology that brings specialist knowledge, advanced analytical methods, fit-for-purpose process models, and AI-driven reasoning together in one environment.

The breadth of engineering science

Bringing the full breadth of engineering science to every investigation.

No small team—or even a team of PhD engineers—can remain expert across every discipline now needed to solve the hardest processing problems.

Mineralogy, minerals processing, equipment design, mine operations, plant operations, data science, machine learning, statistics, causal reasoning, and AI each contribute part of the picture.

Our approach integrates that knowledge so the method can be chosen to suit the engineering problem, rather than the problem being constrained by the tools an engineer happens to know.

MODELING PRINCIPLE

The simplest model capable of answering the question

Different investigations require different levels of fidelity. Models should be practical to maintain and become more detailed only when reliable inference requires it.

REASONING PRINCIPLE

Rigorous reasoning. Traceable conclusions.

Every conclusion should be supported by inspectable evidence, explicit assumptions, reproducible analysis, and a clear account of competing explanations.

Accessible capability

Frontier-level intelligence, wherever the plant operates.

Advanced engineering capability should not depend on having an entire department of specialists on site.

From remote operations to complex processing plants, small teams confront multidisciplinary challenges with finite time and resources.

Our AI Process Engineer is intended to make integrated, up-to-date specialist knowledge and rigorous analytical capability practical for the engineers closest to the problem.
