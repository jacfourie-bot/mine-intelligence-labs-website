Investigations Workbench

Investigations Workbench

Our prototype Investigations Workbench is a tool that works with your team to solve problems. It brings together domain expertise, an understanding of your processing plant, advanced analytical methods, process models, and rigorous scientific reasoning into one problem solving environment.

Powerful capabilities. Conversational interaction. Engineering conclusions you can examine and challenge.

A different kind of engineering system

Built to investigate, not simply answer.

The Investigations Workbench is designed to help engineers structure investigation questions, generate hypotheses, automatically recommend appropriate methods to test the hypothesis, analyze plant data, reason about the results, and develop conclusions grounded in reproducible, auditable analytical work. The system can also recommend steps for additional data collection to verify results or increase confidence in the conclusions.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Frame the engineering problem

Translate operating observations into a structured investigation, with explicit assumptions, uncertainties, and competing explanations.

02 / ANALYZE

Automatically select the best methods

Combine engineering calculations, statistical analysis, machine learning, causal reasoning, and simulation according to the problem.

03 / MODEL

Build fit-for-purpose representations

Use the simplest process model capable of supporting reliable inference, increasing fidelity only where the investigation requires it.

04 / EXPLAIN

Make the analytical path visible

Expose the evidence, calculations, model assumptions, and reasoning behind each conclusion so engineers can scrutinize the result.

The breadth of engineering science

Bringing the full breadth of engineering science to every investigation.

No small team—or even a team of PhD engineers—can remain expert across every discipline now needed to solve the hardest processing problems.

Mineralogy, minerals processing, equipment design, mine operations, plant operations, data science, machine learning, statistics, causal reasoning, and AI each contribute part of the picture.

Our approach integrates that knowledge so the method can be chosen to suit the engineering problem, rather than the problem being constrained by the tools an engineer happens to know.

Designed for the engineer

Direct the work with a conversation, just like you would do with any expert team member.

Engineers can direct the investigation through natural interaction, challenge conclusions, introduce new evidence, and determine what should be tested next.

The objective is not autonomous decision-making. It is a more capable engineering partnership.
