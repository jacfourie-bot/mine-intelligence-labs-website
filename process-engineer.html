AI Process Engineer

The next generation of minerals processing intelligence.

Our prototype AI Process Engineer is a tool that works with your team to solve problems. It brings together domain expertise, an understanding of your processing plant, advanced analytical methods, process models, and rigorous scientific reasoning into one problem solving environment.

Powerful capabilities. Natural interaction. Engineering conclusions you can examine and challenge.

A different kind of engineering system

Built to investigate, not simply answer.

The AI Process Engineer is designed to help engineers structure difficult questions, generate and test hypotheses, automatically recommend appropriate methods, examine evidence, and develop conclusions grounded in reproducible, auditable analytical work.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Frame the engineering problem

Translate operating observations into a structured investigation, with explicit assumptions, uncertainties, and competing explanations.

02 / ANALYZE

Automatically select the best methods

Combine engineering calculations, statistical analysis, machine learning, causal reasoning, and simulation according to the problem.

03 / MODEL

Build fit-for-purpose representations

Use the simplest process model capable of supporting reliable inference, increasing fidelity only where the investigation requires it.

04 / EXPLAIN

Make the analytical path visible

Expose the evidence, calculations, model assumptions, and reasoning behind each conclusion so engineers can scrutinize the result.

Designed for the engineer

Direct the work with a conversation, just like you would do with any expert team member.

Engineers can direct the investigation through natural interaction, challenge conclusions, introduce new evidence, and determine what should be tested next.

The objective is not autonomous decision-making. It is a more capable engineering partnership.
