Mine Intelligence Labs / Research-led industrial AI

Advancing the science of how minerals processing problems are solved.

Minerals processing plants are complex, dynamic systems. Mineralogy, mine planning, mining selectivity, process control, equipment condition, and human decision making all contribute to overall mine performance. Despite the use of tools such as APC and online analysis in modern plants, even well-staffed and highly experienced engineering teams encounter problems that resist conventional analysis.

Recent advances in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computational methods make a fundamentally new approach possible. Mine Intelligence Labs is a startup researching the frontier of these approaches and developing tools to help process engineering teams accelerate their improvement cycle.

Our first product, the AI Process Engineer, acts as an expert team member, combining engineering knowledge, plant flowsheets, plant data, advanced analytical methods, and process models to conduct auditable, repeatable investigations and identify root causes. Problems that have historically been prohibitively time-consuming, difficult, or expensive to solve can now be tackled.

Whether you are looking to accelerate improvements, or working on the industry's most difficult problems, we would like to collaborate with you.

Contact us at contact@mineintelligencelabs.com.
