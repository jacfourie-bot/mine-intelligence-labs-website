Applications

Investigating the problems that resist conventional analysis.

The AI Process Engineer is being developed to support complex, multidisciplinary investigations across mineral processing operations. These examples illustrate intended applications, not completed case studies.

Illustrative investigations

From symptoms to underlying causes.

01

Declining flotation recovery

Separate the influence of ore characteristics, liberation, reagent regimes, hydrodynamics, instrumentation, and operating practice.

02

Grinding circuit instability

Evaluate interacting effects across feed variability, classification, mill loading, control behavior, and equipment condition.

03

Throughput constraints

Identify where production is truly constrained and distinguish persistent bottlenecks from shifting, context-dependent limitations.

04

Equipment degradation

Connect condition indicators, process behavior, and operating context to investigate performance loss and emerging failure modes.

05

Unexplained performance variability

Test competing explanations for changes in recovery, quality, energy intensity, stability, and operating response.

The objective

Make previously impractical investigations practical.

The value lies not in applying a single algorithm everywhere, but in selecting and combining the engineering and analytical methods appropriate to each question.
